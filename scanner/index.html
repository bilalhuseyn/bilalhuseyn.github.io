LegalScan scanner information

LegalScan is an automated website checker that is still in development. This is the page its identification strings point to. It is a temporary address; it will move to the product's own website once that exists, and this page will then point there.

How it identifies itself

  • Website checks: the real Chromium browser string, followed by LegalScan/0.1.0 (+https://bilalhuseyn.github.io/scanner/). Nothing in the string is hidden or disguised.
  • Archive research: LegalScan-research/0.1 (+https://bilalhuseyn.github.io/scanner/), used only when downloading public files from the Common Crawl archive.

What it does today

  • It checks a website only when the site's owner asked for the check and showed control of the domain (a DNS TXT record, a meta tag or a file under /.well-known/). These checks are currently run by an operator, by hand, from one computer.
  • On such a site it opens, with plain page loads in a browser, the landing page, the legal and information pages the site links to, the pages the owner listed, and a few common information addresses (for example /refund-policy). It looks at what the pages show and which requests and cookies appear, with and without a click on the cookie banner's own buttons, if there is one.
  • It reads robots.txt first and follows it. The product token is LegalScan.
  • For archive research it reads pages that Common Crawl has already archived. It sends no request to the archived websites. A page whose archived copy carries a text-and-data-mining reservation, or that the archived robots.txt disallows, is skipped. No list of the sites in the sample is published.

What it does not do

  • It does not submit forms, log in, register, search, buy, download files from a site or solve challenges.
  • It does not try to get around bot protection, blocks or robots.txt. If it is blocked, it stops and records "Could not check"; it never retries from another network or location.
  • It does not contact the owners of the sites it looks at.
  • It does not judge, score or certify a site. The output of a website check is a list of observations ("Signal observed", "No signal found", "Depends on your situation", "Could not check") for the owner who asked for it. It is not legal advice.

What it keeps

  • Website checks: raw captures are deleted within 24 hours; the result is kept for the owner who asked for the check. No full-page screenshots are kept.
  • Archive research: raw archive records are deleted within 24 hours and derived observations within 7 days; only counts across many sites are used, never a result about one site.
  • No personal data is kept, and page text is never sent to a language model.

Contact and opt-out

To ask a question, or to ask that your site is not visited, open an issue at github.com/bilalhuseyn/bilalhuseyn.github.io/issues. A site whose owner asks us not to visit it will not be visited.
