Help: how to cancel a subscription

Fictional help text. There are no accounts on this site, so there is nothing to cancel.

If this were a real service, you would cancel in three steps:

  1. Open the account settings.
  2. Choose the billing section and select your plan.
  3. Pick the option to stop renewal. The plan keeps running until the end of the paid period.

Refunds and renewal dates are described in the terms of service. Questions: see the contact page.
